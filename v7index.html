Product

OTIS Establishes Physical Evidence Where Signals, GPS, and Pixels Fail.

Modern sensing systems detect, classify, track, and fuse what their sensors observe. Their outputs are trained on enormous image datasets, weighted by confidence, and relied upon to shape the operational picture.

That architecture has a structural gap: a weighted optical observation can enter the decision chain without first demonstrating that the observation adheres to the laws of physics.

Decision pollution begins when false, manipulated, degraded, or physically unproven observations enter the operational picture and consume compute, communications bandwidth, operator attention, time, and interceptor inventory.

OTIS is not another classifier, confidence score, or fusion weight. OTIS is the physics authority that sets the operational standard for optical evidence.

Observations that adhere to the laws of matter and motion may proceed into downstream workflows. Geometry-failed optical evidence is hard-zeroed before the evidence can alter the mission.

No adherence to physics. No operational consequence.
Threats

The Threats That Silence, Degrade, or Weaponize Conventional Sensors.

Fiber-optic guided munitions, glide threats, RF-silent autonomous drones, GPS-spoofed craft, and adversarial optical threats each remove, degrade, corrupt, or weaponize a conventional confirmation pathway.

OTIS applies the remaining standard: whether the observed target adheres to the strict laws of matter and motion.

01

Fiber-Optic Guided Munitions

Fiber-optic guided systems can operate without RF control links, GPS dependence, or usable electronic signatures. OTIS applies physics to the target information available through the optical sensor.

02

Glide Threats

Glide threats can enter critical terminal phases without a propulsion signature. OTIS evaluates adherence to the strict laws of matter and motion.

03

RF-Silent Autonomous Drones

Autonomous platforms can navigate and execute without active telemetry. OTIS determines whether passive optical evidence can drive downstream workflows.

04

GPS-Spoofed Craft

Manipulated or degraded position and intent cannot be treated as ground truth. OTIS applies the physical standard to the optical observation.

05

Adversarial Optical Spoofing

Camouflage, patches, and optical manipulation change pixels. Adversarial presentation cannot change the physical constraints that matter must obey.

06

FlyTrap-Class Printed Spoofs

Published physical attacks show that image-led systems can accept evidence that does not adhere to physical reality. FlyTrap is one proof point; decision pollution is the full problem class.
