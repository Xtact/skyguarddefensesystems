The Solution

Current EO/IR systems rely on AI, ML, and computer-vision image libraries to recognize what a threat looks like — an approach adversaries can defeat for the cost of a printed pattern.

OTIS applies ground-truth physics and geometry instead. Accuracy comes from Newton's fundamental laws, not learned pixels — freeing up the compute those AI/ML pipelines demand, and letting OTIS run autonomously beyond the edge in contested, DDIL environments.

The OTIS Difference

OTIS operates differently. Adversarial patches, novel airframes, and electronic countermeasures change pixels. They cannot change physics.
