The product

Trust begins below the classifier.

OTIS is the geometry intelligence layer that verifies whether an observed target remains consistent with the physical world before its optical signal can inform a safety-critical decision.

01 / VERIFY

Geometry-first

Physical plausibility, structural continuity, and track behavior form the basis for trusted optical sensing.

02 / GATE

Binary decision control

When geometry fails, unverified optical features do not receive a softer weight; they are excluded at the decision gate.

03 / CUE

Platform-ready software

OTIS is designed as an integration layer for passive sensing and autonomy platforms rather than as a standalone hardware product.

Threat spectrum

The threats conventional signals do not reveal.

RF-dependent and appearance-led systems have fundamental gaps against the most consequential classes of passive, signal-denied threats. OTIS is designed to establish geometry-based trust across that operating environment.

THREAT / 01

Fiber-optic guided munitions

No RF link, no GPS dependence, and no detectable telemetry path for signal-led detection architectures.

THREAT / 02

Glide threats

Terminal trajectories can arrive without a propulsion signature that conventional detection systems expect.

THREAT / 03

RF-silent autonomous drones

Onboard navigation and autonomous operation remove the telemetry assumptions embedded in many counter-UAS approaches.

THREAT / 04+

Adversarial navigation and optics

GPS-spoofed craft and optical manipulation exploit the difference between an image-derived claim and a physically verified target.

Innovation

A physics-grounded trust gate.

OTIS evaluates target consistency through geometric and kinematic constraints, then applies a hard-zero decision rule when that verification fails. The point is not higher confidence in pixels; it is preventing unverified pixels from controlling the decision.

ARCHITECTURE / 01

Optical input

Early-stage adversarial hardening can operate on optical input, including monocular sensing configurations.

ARCHITECTURE / 02

Depth resolution

Depth-resolving inputs enable live volumetric verification through compatible stereo, time-of-flight, LiDAR, structured light, or equivalent methods.

ARCHITECTURE / 03

Target cueing

Verified spatial structure supports persistent tracking and target cueing for the platform-level mission.
