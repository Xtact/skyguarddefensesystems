Innovation

Pixels Suggest. Physics Sets the Standard.

An image can be altered. A pixel pattern can be designed to deceive. Training data can be incomplete. A weighted model can be highly confident and still be wrong. Fusion can spread that error across the operational picture.

OTIS does not sit beside those systems as another opinion. It is the authority that determines whether image-derived evidence crosses into operational consequence.
